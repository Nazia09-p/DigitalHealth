About The Project

The Human Adaptive Cyber Physical is an advanced healthcare analytics system designed to predict the likelihood of activties using machine learning algorithms. The objective is early detection, faster diagnosis, and smarter decision-making for patients.

Our Mission

To use technology and artificial intelligence to help detect activities early, reduce risks, and support medical professionals with accurate predictions.

Why This Project?

  • Increase prediction accuracy using ML models
  • Provide real-time risk assessment
  • Help hospitals and individuals detect diseases earlier
  • Support healthcare automation
